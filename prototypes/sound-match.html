What changed. Matched cards are now hollow — the fill drops and the note's colour becomes an outline. Dimming them was never going to read against a board full of face-up cards under Stay up.

Two bugs you found. Eight pairs of a seven-note scale wrapped round, so two different pairs were both a red C; an octave up is now the same colour lightened, with a mark on the letter. And a card's note ran on for up to 4.8 s, so the second card of a pair was heard through the first — which is why matching cards did not sound the same. A note now damps the one before it.
