Why this page exists
Nothing tells you what a MIDI device IS. A port gives you a name, a manufacturer and an id — there is no field that says "drum pad". So the only honest way to know is to watch what it plays and adapt, which has the happy side effect of working for a device nobody has heard of.

This bench does that classification out loud so the rule can be judged before anything is built on top of it.
